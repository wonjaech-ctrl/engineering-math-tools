Matrix form

Each row of A becomes one equation. The corresponding entry in b becomes the right-hand side of that equation.

Solution

Key idea: Writing Ax = b does not create a new problem. We simply collect the coefficients into matrix A, the unknowns into vector x, and the right-hand-side values into vector b.
